# Mama Guard AI Product Strategy

## 1. AI Positioning
Mama Guard Assistant is not an AI doctor. It is an AI-assisted maternal safety companion that helps users understand warning signs, organize symptom information, prepare care summaries, and know when to contact a healthcare provider.

## 2. What AI Can Safely Do
- Explain warning signs in simple language
- Help users summarize symptoms for providers
- Personalize education based on pregnancy/postpartum stage
- Help build a safety plan
- Help users prepare questions for healthcare providers
- Later, help health workers prioritize consented cases

## 3. What AI Must Not Do
- Diagnose
- Prescribe medication
- Replace healthcare professionals
- Claim clinical approval
- Contact emergency services automatically
- Monitor real patients without consent and secure backend
- Make decisions without human involvement

## 4. AI MVP Features
Practical early features:
- Red-flag symptom assistant
- Provider summary generator
- Safety plan builder
- Educational article recommender
- Check-in explanation assistant
- Postpartum warning sign support

## 5. Future AI Features
- Voice symptom check-in
- Multilingual support such as English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo
- Provider dashboard risk prioritization
- Follow-up reminders
- AI-generated care questions for appointments

## 6. Data and Safety Requirements Before Real AI Launch
- User consent
- Secure backend
- Supabase Auth or equivalent
- Row Level Security
- Privacy policy
- Medical review of content
- Human escalation workflow
- Audit logs
- Testing with real users and maternal health workers

## 7. Investor Story
How AI makes Mama Guard stronger:
- Improves maternal safety awareness
- Helps mothers communicate symptoms clearly
- Supports early warning detection
- Reduces confusion during pregnancy/postpartum
- Can support community health workers in the future

## 8. Immediate Next Build Recommendation
We recommend starting with AI-assisted provider summary and red-flag explanation, using safe rule-based logic first, before connecting any paid AI API.
